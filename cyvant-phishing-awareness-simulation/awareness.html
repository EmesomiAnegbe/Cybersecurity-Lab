🚨 5 Phishing Indicators You Should Have Spotted

01

Urgent & Threatening Language

The email used phrases like "Immediate Action Required" and "Your account will be suspended" to create panic and rush your decision.

02

Suspicious URL / Web Address

The real Cyvant portal URL is portal.cyvanttech.com. Always check the address bar before entering credentials anywhere.

03

Unexpected Login Request

You were not expecting this email. Legitimate systems rarely ask you to re-enter credentials through an emailed link without prior notice.

04

No Security Certificate Verification

Always check for the padlock icon 🔒 in the browser address bar. A missing or invalid certificate is a major red flag on any login page.

05

Generic Greeting & Sender Mismatch

The email used "Dear Employee" instead of your name. Also check the sender's actual email address — not just the display name.
